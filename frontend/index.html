About GovEase

Academic project

GovEase is a student major project that brings three services together: an assistant for understanding government information, a feed of official government updates, and a tool for preparing documents for online applications.

The Government Updates module collects items from a small set of official government RSS feeds, cleans and categorises them, stores them, and links every item back to the department that published it. GovEase does not issue government notifications and is not a substitute for the official source.
