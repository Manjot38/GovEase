Smart Document & Form Tool

Documents & Forms

Get your photograph, signature and certificates ready for online government applications — resize, compress, convert and merge in one place.

Files are processed in memory and discarded as soon as the result is sent. Nothing is stored.

Ready for application

Choose what the form asks for. GovEase resizes, compresses and converts your file to match, then checks it.

Limits differ between forms. Always confirm the size and format in the official notification.

Resize image

Leave width or height empty to keep the original proportions.

Centimetres are converted to pixels using the DPI value (e.g. 3.5 cm at 300 DPI = 413 px).

Compress to a target size

Works for images and PDFs. GovEase finds the best quality that fits under your limit.

Convert

Conversion

Pages appear in the order you select the files. Multi-page PDFs download as a ZIP of images.

Merge PDFs

Combine marksheets, certificates and ID proofs into the single PDF many forms ask for.
